Same shell, three bolder identities

The floating, canvas-first layout from the last round stays as it was. What changes here is type and colour. Each identity has a light and a dark theme, and the switch on each mockup works.

A · JetstreamB · RisoC · Marquee

A · Jetstream

Speed as the brand. Wide italic capitals, everything banked six degrees, ultramarine and tangerine.

The welcome deck at its overview stop

Type

Archivo, one family. Expanded black italic for the wordmark and titles, regular width for the interface, so the app needs a single bundled font.

Colour

Light mode writes in ultramarine ink on white, not black. Dark mode is deep ultramarine, not grey. Tangerine is the only action colour in both.

Signature

The six-degree bank. The wordmark, the slabs and the speed stripes share one angle, and the camera levels out as it flies into each slide. The Present button leans the same way.

B · Riso

Printed, not rendered. Three inks, square corners, and outlines that sit slightly off their shapes like a misregistered print.

The welcome deck at its overview stop

Type

Syne for the wordmark, titles and labels: wide, a little odd, instantly recognisable. Onest for interface text, which stays quiet at 13px.

Colour

Blue ink, fluorescent pink and yellow on paper. Dark mode floods the page with the blue ink and prints in paper white. There is no black or grey anywhere.

Signature

The offset. Panels cast a hard pink shadow, shapes carry an outline shifted a few pixels, and the wordmark has a pink ghost. All of it is two flat elements, so it ships with what the renderer has today.

C · Marquee

A presentation is a show. Emerald and blush, a soft serif in italics, and slides that stand on a stage as a row of arches.

The welcome deck at its overview stop

Type

Fraunces for titles, the deck name and panel labels, with the italic doing the moving. Figtree for interface text.

Colour

Dark mode is emerald with blush text. Light mode swaps them: blush ground, emerald ink. Marigold is the action colour in both.

Signature

The arch. Feature slides are arches on a stage line, the logo is an arch, and controls are fully round. It is the warmest of the three and the furthest from a typical software look.

My pick

Jetstream. It is the only one whose idea comes from the product's name and its one unique feature, the moving camera. It also needs one font file, and its dark theme being blue instead of grey is something users will remember.

Riso is the most original on screen. Marquee is the most charming, but it would suit a consumer storytelling tool better than one that imports PowerPoint decks.

All three fonts are open-licence and can ship inside the app. None of them covers Hebrew, Arabic or CJK, so those scripts keep today's system-font fallback in every option.
